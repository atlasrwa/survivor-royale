# Survivor Royale - Complete Feature Summary

## 🎯 Core Concept
Skill-based auto-shooter with **3 deep heroes**, **30+ unique enemies**, **2-player co-op**, and **Web3 integration**. Think Survivor.io meets Hades with competitive multiplayer and true NFT ownership.

---

## 🎭 The Three Heroes (Deep Customization)

### Why 3 Heroes Instead of Many?
- **Depth over breadth**: Each hero has 4 evolution tiers with branching choices
- **Mastery focus**: Players "main" a hero, creating identity
- **Content quality**: 50+ cosmetic NFTs per hero (150+ total)
- **Balance easier**: 3 well-tuned heroes vs 10 broken ones

---

### 🗡️ KNIGHT - The Melee Tank

**Fantasy**: Unstoppable juggernaut who thrives in chaos

**Base Stats**:
- Highest HP (150)
- Slowest speed (90%)
- Starting weapon: Greatsword (cleaving arc)

**Signature Ability**: "Iron Resolve" (Q, 15s cooldown)
- **Tier 1** (Start): Shield absorbs 50 damage for 3s
- **Tier 2** (Wave 10): Shield 100 damage, reflects 50% blocked damage
- **Tier 3** (Wave 20): Shield 5s, grants speed boost, explodes on end
- **Tier 4** (Wave 30): Auto-cast when low HP, adds lifesteal

**Skill Tree** (Choose 1 per tier):
- **Level 3**: Berserker (+30% damage, -5% HP) OR Guardian (+40% HP, -10% damage) OR Duelist (+15% attack speed, crit lifesteal)
- **Level 8**: Earthquake Slam (every 5th attack = AOE) OR Blood Armor (damage = stacks) OR Whirlwind (spin to win)
- **Level 15**: **ULTIMATE** - Titan Form (10s transformation, +100% damage, immune knockback, 60s cooldown)
- **Level 25**: Immortal (revive once) OR Execute (instakill <20% HP enemies) OR War Cry (buff allies in co-op)

**Build Examples**:
- Berserker + Whirlwind + Execute = AOE monster who executes weakened enemies
- Guardian + Blood Armor + Immortal = Unkillable raid boss
- Duelist + Earthquake + War Cry = Co-op support DPS

**Playstyle**: Dive deep, survive through sustain, zone control

---

### 🏹 ARCHER - The Glass Cannon

**Fantasy**: Precision striker who dominates through positioning

**Base Stats**:
- Lowest HP (100)
- Fastest speed (110%)
- Starting weapon: Longbow (piercing arrows)

**Signature Ability**: "Perfect Shot" (Q, 12s cooldown)
- **Tier 1** (Start): Slow time 50%, click to fire 300% damage shot
- **Tier 2** (Wave 10): Shot pierces all enemies, longer time slow
- **Tier 3** (Wave 20): Fire up to 3 shots during slow, can crit
- **Tier 4** (Wave 30): If all 3 hit, instant cooldown refresh

**Skill Tree**:
- **Level 3**: Explosive Arrows (AOE on hit) OR Poison Arrows (DoT) OR Multi-Shot (+2 arrows, less damage)
- **Level 8**: Dodge Roll (spacebar, i-frames) OR Shadow Step (teleport back when hit) OR Wind Walker (+20% speed, slow trail)
- **Level 15**: **ULTIMATE** - Arrow Storm (50 rapid arrows for 8s, 45s cooldown)
- **Level 25**: Headshot (15% instakill) OR Chain Lightning (arrows spread electricity) OR Hunter's Mark (tag enemy for +50% team damage)

**Build Examples**:
- Explosive + Dodge Roll + Headshot = AOE burst assassin
- Poison + Wind Walker + Chain Lightning = Kiting DoT master
- Multi-Shot + Shadow Step + Hunter's Mark = Co-op boss killer

**Playstyle**: Constant movement, precision timing, high risk/reward

---

### 🔮 MAGE - The Elemental Master

**Fantasy**: Battlefield controller who bends elements to their will

**Base Stats**:
- Medium HP (120)
- Average speed (100%)
- Starting weapon: Arcane Missiles (homing)

**Signature Ability**: "Elemental Shift" (Q, 18s cooldown)
- **Tier 1** (Start): Toggle Fire/Ice/Lightning stance
  - Fire: +20% damage, burn
  - Ice: Slow enemies 30%
  - Lightning: 10% chain to nearby
- **Tier 2** (Wave 10): Stronger bonuses, switching creates explosion
- **Tier 3** (Wave 20): Combine 2 elements (Fire+Ice=Steam, Fire+Lightning=Plasma, Ice+Lightning=Blizzard)
- **Tier 4** (Wave 30): All 3 elements active, passive damage aura

**Skill Tree**:
- **Level 3**: Pyromancer (fire bonus) OR Cryomancer (ice bonus) OR Stormcaller (lightning bonus)
- **Level 8**: Meteor (periodic huge AOE) OR Blink (teleport + AOE) OR Mana Shield (damage → mana drain)
- **Level 15**: **ULTIMATE** - Cataclysm (massive elemental explosion, 60s cooldown)
- **Level 25**: Archmage (all spells free for 10s) OR Elemental Fusion (3 spells = combo explosion) OR Mana Overflow (spells drop healing orbs)

**Build Examples**:
- Pyromancer + Meteor + Archmage = Nuclear option (spam meteors)
- Cryomancer + Blink + Elemental Fusion = Mobile combo mage
- Stormcaller + Mana Shield + Mana Overflow = Sustain battle-mage

**Playstyle**: Area control, combo execution, strategic stance swapping

## 👾 Enemy System (30+ Unique Types)

### Design Principles:
- **Visual clarity**: Color-coded threats (red=melee, purple=ranged, yellow=elite)
- **Counter-play**: Every enemy weak to specific elements/tactics
- **Variety**: New enemy types unlock deeper strategies
- **Scaling**: Co-op mode adds +75% HP, +50% count, new mechanics

### Enemy Roster:

**Waves 1-10 (Basic Threats)**:
1. Zombies - Slow melee swarm
2. Runners - Fast, low HP
3. Shooters - Ranged basic
4. Tanks - Block projectiles from front
5. Exploders - Suicide bombers

**Waves 11-20 (Advanced Threats)**:
6. Summoners - Spawn adds (priority target)
7. Teleporters - Blink behind player
8. Shielded Knights - Directional immunity
9. Necromancers - Resurrect dead enemies
10. Berserkers - Gain damage/speed over time
11. Snipers - Laser-sight one-shots
12. Healers - Buff nearby enemies

**Waves 21-30 (Elite Threats)**:
13. Shadow Stalkers - Stealth assassins
14. Juggernauts - CC-immune, charge attacks
15. Witches - Debuff casters
16. Swarm Hosts - Constant mini-spawns
17. Mimics - Clone player appearance
18. Void Reapers - Drain XP on hit
19. Constructs - Armored with weak spots
20. Portals - Invulnerable spawn points

**Waves 30+ (Nightmare Threats)**:
21. Champions - Random 2 elite abilities
22. Chaos Beasts - Abilities change every 5s
23. Swarm Queens - Endless spawn until killed

### Boss Fights (Every 10 Waves):

**Wave 10 - "The Titan"**:
- Tank check
- Telegraphed charges (dodge perpendicular)
- Phase 2: Double charges
- Weak spot: Back

**Wave 20 - "Shadow Assassin"**:
- Mobility check
- Teleports + dagger barrages
- Phase 2: Splits into 3 clones (find real one)
- Phase 3: Berserk mode (DPS race)

**Wave 30 - "Rift Lord"**:
- All-skills check
- **Phase 1**: Element cycling (immune to current element)
- **Phase 2**: Summons Juggernauts + Necromancers
- **Phase 3**: All elements + shrinking arena
- **Phase 4**: Invulnerable until you kill 20 Champions
- Hardest challenge in game

## 🎮 CO-OP MODE (The Killer Feature)

### Why Co-Op Will Dominate:

**What Survivor.io Lacks**:
- No multiplayer whatsoever
- Players have begged for co-op for years
- Community mods exist trying to add multiplayer

**Our Opportunity**:
- First major auto-shooter with native co-op
- Built-in from launch (not tacked on)
- Deep synergy systems (not just "2 players shoot things")

---

### Co-Op Mechanics:

**Core Systems**:
- **2 players** (no more - keeps it tight)
- **Separate health bars** (individual survivability)
- **Revive system**: Dead player drops body, partner has 10s to reach and revive (3s channel)
- **Shared XP**: All kills give XP to both players
- **Individual builds**: Each player levels and chooses upgrades independently
- **Proximity bonus**: Stay within 10m → both gain +5% damage (visual tether)

**Hero Synergies**:

| Combo | Bonus Effect |
|-------|--------------|
| Knight + Mage | Knight's melee applies Mage's current element |
| Knight + Archer | Knight gains +10% damage per nearby enemy |
| Archer + Mage | Archer arrows trigger Mage's elemental explosions |

**Ultimate Combos** (Cast within 2s of each other):
- **Knight + Archer**: Arrow Storm pierces shields
- **Knight + Mage**: Titan Form gains elemental damage aura
- **Archer + Mage**: Cataclysm fires 20 seeking missiles
