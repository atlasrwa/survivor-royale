# Survivor Royale - Game Design Document

## High-Level Concept

A skill-based, browser-playable auto-shooter where players survive increasingly difficult waves of enemies. Pure skill determines success - cosmetics and weapon variety are monetized, but all weapons are balanced. Web3 integration rewards loyal players through airdrops and exclusive cosmetics.

**Target**: Crypto-native gamers who want competitive, skill-based gameplay
**Session Length**: 10-20 minutes per run
**Platform**: Web (desktop & mobile browsers)

## 🎯 KEY DIFFERENTIATORS (Why Players Choose This Over Survivor.io)

### 1. **True Competitive Ladder (Not Just High Scores)**

**Survivor.io problem**: Leaderboards are static, no real competition
**Our solution**: 
- **Real-time ranked seasons** with live leaderboards
- **Skill rating system (ELO-based)** - play against similarly skilled opponents
- **Bracket tournaments** every weekend with live spectating
- **Guild vs Guild seasons** - coordinate with teammates for collective ranking
- Your success directly competes with others for token prizes

### 2. **Evolving Meta Game (Weekly Balance Changes)**

**Survivor.io problem**: Stale meta, same builds dominate forever
**Our solution**:
- **Weekly balance patches** voted by community
- **Featured weapon rotations** - certain combos get bonuses each week
- **Meta shifts** keep the game fresh (forces new strategies)
- **Community-driven balance** - Discord vote on weapon buffs/nerfs
- **Seasonal modifiers** - "Melee Month", "Speed Season", etc.

### 3. **Social & Spectator Features**

**Survivor.io problem**: Single-player only, no social interaction
**Our solution**:
- **Live spectator mode** - watch top players in real-time
- **"Ghost runs"** - race against friend's recorded run simultaneously
- **Co-op mode (future)** - 2 players, shared screen, harder enemies
- **Build sharing** - import someone's exact upgrade path with a code
- **Tournament viewing** - Twitch-style viewing of finals with chat
- **Creator tools** - Streamers get special overlays, alerts for viewers

### 4. **Dynamic Content & Events**

**Survivor.io problem**: Same content for years
**Our solution**:
- **Limited-time events** (2-3 per month):
  - "Boss Rush" - only boss fights, no normal waves
  - "One Weapon Challenge" - restricted builds
  - "Speed Run Mode" - race to Wave 20 fastest
  - "Chaos Mode" - random modifiers each wave
- **Seasonal bosses** with exclusive NFT drops for first-time killers
- **Community challenges** - "Collectively kill 1M enemies this week" for rewards
- **Holiday events** with themed enemies and exclusive cosmetics

### 5. **Skill Expression Mechanics**

**Survivor.io problem**: Mostly build RNG, less player skill
**Our solution**:
- **Manual dodge roll** (cooldown-based, i-frames) - rewards timing
- **Active abilities** per character (Q key, 15s cooldown):
  - Scout: Dash with speed boost
  - Tank: Temporary shield
  - Mage: AOE spell burst
  - Hunter: Aimed shot (pause time briefly, click target)
- **Combo system** - chaining kills fast gives XP multiplier
- **Perfect dodge** - dodging at last second gives temporary damage boost
- Separates good players from great players

### 6. **Transparent Progression & No RNG Gating**

**Survivor.io problem**: Paywalled characters, grindy unlocks
**Our solution**:
- **All gameplay content unlockable in ~20 hours**
- **No loot boxes** - you know exactly what you're buying
- **Clear upgrade paths** - see what unlocks at each level
- **Account level gives tangible rewards** - not just cosmetics
- Wallet connect gives instant access to trading (turn skins to profit)

### 7. **Web3-Native Features (Not Tacked On)**

**What makes this actually Web3**:
- **Every cosmetic is an NFT** (instant marketplace liquidity)
- **Portable identity** - your wallet is your account (no email needed)
- **Provable fairness** - all ranked matches recorded on-chain
- **Token-gated modes** unlock competitive play (skin in the game)
- **Airdrop rewards for skill** - top players earn passively
- **Creator royalties** - artists earn % of secondary sales forever
- **DAO governance** - community votes on game direction

### 8. **Better Game Feel & Polish**

**Technical differentiation**:
- **Smoother controls** - 60 FPS guaranteed, responsive movement
- **Better visual feedback** - damage numbers, hit effects, screen shake
- **Satisfying audio** - punchy weapon sounds, bass on explosions
- **Particle effects** - over-the-top visual spectacle (like Hades)
- **Accessibility options** - colorblind modes, UI scaling, control remapping
- **Performance mode** - runs on potato PCs/phones

### 9. **Community-First Development**

**How we build differently**:
- **Public roadmap** on Discord - vote on next features
- **Weekly dev logs** - transparency builds trust
- **Creator program** - partner with streamers/YouTubers for content
- **Bounty program** - community submits weapon ideas for tokens
- **Bug bounties** - find exploits, get rewarded not banned
- **Open economy data** - marketplace stats, token metrics public

### 10. **Actual Viral Mechanics**

**Built-in growth loops**:
- **Referral system** - invite friend, both get exclusive NFT when they wallet connect
- **Clip sharing** - auto-highlight reels of best moments (kills, dodges, bosses)
- **Twitter integration** - auto-post victories with custom graphics
- **Streamer drops** - watch partnered streamers, get in-game drops
- **Challenge codes** - "Beat my run with this build" → shareable links
- **Guild recruitment** - invite links with guild cosmetics preview

## Core Gameplay Loop

### Basic Mechanics

**Movement & Combat**:
- WASD/Arrow keys or touch controls for movement
- Auto-fire weapons (Survivor.io style)
- Character auto-targets nearest enemy
- Dodge-focused gameplay - positioning is everything

**Wave System**:
- Endless waves of enemies
- Difficulty scales infinitely
- Every 5 waves = mini-boss
- Every 10 waves = major boss with unique mechanics
- Goal: Survive as long as possible

**Leveling During Run**:
- Kill enemies → earn XP
- Level up → choose 1 of 3 random upgrades
- Upgrades include:
  - New weapons (stack up to 6 active weapons)
  - Weapon evolutions (combine 2 weapons at max level)
  - Stat boosts (HP, speed, pickup range, etc.)
  - Passive abilities (lifesteal, thorns, shields, etc.)

**Win Condition**:
- Survive to Wave 30 = "Victory" (game continues but you've "won")
- Death = game over, score recorded
- Leaderboard based on: (Waves survived × 100) + (Enemies killed)

### Free-to-Play Progression

**Account Leveling**:

**Achievement System**:
- 100+ achievements for various milestones
- Displayed on player profile

## Monetization (Wallet-Only)

### Cosmetic Currency ("Essence")
- Earned through gameplay (challenges, leveling, achievements)
- **Can ONLY be purchased with crypto (USDC/ETH/MATIC)**
- Wallet connection required for all purchases
- **Never impacts gameplay power**

### What You Can Buy (Wallet Required):

**Character Skins** (250-1000 Essence):
- Visual variants of base characters
- Animated effects, particle trails
- Rare skins from events/challenges
- Purchase with crypto → receive tradeable NFT

**Weapon Skins** (150-500 Essence):
- Visual changes to weapon effects
- Custom colors, projectile shapes
- Does NOT change stats
- Also minted as NFTs

**Battle Pass** (10 USDC or equivalent/season, ~60 days):
- Paid ONLY via wallet connect
- Free track: Basic rewards
- Premium track: Exclusive NFT skins, emotes, essence
- Progress by playing (skill-based challenges accelerate)
- All cosmetic, zero gameplay advantage

**Starter Packs** (One-time wallet purchases):
- 5 USDC: 2 exclusive NFT skins + 300 Essence
- 10 USDC: 5 NFT skins + character unlock + 800 Essence

**Why wallet-only:**
- Forces Web3 adoption from day 1
- All cosmetics are tradeable NFTs (instant marketplace)
- Lower fees than credit card processing
- Builds crypto-native community from start

**Token Launch**: Month 3-4 (after proving traction)

**Token Utility**:

1. **Ranked Seasons** (Monthly):
   - Stake 100 $RIFT to enter ranked mode
   - Top 100 players split 70% of staked pool
   - 20% burned, 10% to treasury
   - Skill-based leaderboard (can't buy rank)

2. **Exclusive Cosmetic Drops**:
   - Limited NFT skins sold for $RIFT
   - Tradeable on marketplace (5% royalty to treasury)
   - Animated legendaries, collabs with artists

3. **Tournament Entry**:
   - Weekend tournaments: 50 $RIFT entry
   - Winner-takes-40%, top 10 split remaining 60%
   - Live-streamed finals

4. **Governance** (Future):
   - Vote on new game modes
   - Vote on weapon balance changes
   - Propose new features

5. **Staking Rewards**:
   - Stake $RIFT → earn "Premium Essence" (faster cosmetic unlocks)
   - Not tradeable, just speeds up free progression

### Airdrop Strategy

**Criteria for airdrops**:
- Top 1000 players by season end
- Players who achieved specific milestones (Wave 30 clear, 10,000 total kills)
- Discord/community contributors
- Early adopters (first 5,000 wallet connects)

**Airdrop amounts**: 
- Tiered based on engagement (not just wallet size)
- Skill-based rewards (top performers get most)

### NFT Cosmetics

**What's an NFT vs What's Not**:
- Regular skins: In-game items (tradeable essence between accounts? No)
- NFT skins: Limited edition, tradeable on marketplace
- NFT benefits:
  - Provable rarity/ownership
  - Can be sold/traded freely
  - Some NFTs give passive Essence generation (small amounts)

**Marketplace**:
- In-game marketplace for NFT trading
- 5% fee (2.5% burn, 2.5% treasury)
- Artists can submit designs, earn royalties from sales

## Hero System (3 Core Classes with Deep Progression)

### Design Philosophy
Instead of many shallow characters, we have 3 deeply customizable heroes. Each hero has:
- Unique starting weapon
- Signature ability (evolves 3 times during run)
- Class-specific skill tree (choose path during run)
- Ultimate ability (unlocks at Wave 15)
- Cosmetic customization (50+ skins per hero)

---

### 🗡️ KNIGHT (Melee Tank)

**Identity**: Close-range juggernaut who gets stronger the longer they survive

**Starting Stats**:
- HP: 150 (highest)
- Speed: 90% (slowest)
- Starting Weapon: **Greatsword** (wide arc melee swing, cleaves enemies)

**Signature Ability: "Iron Resolve"** (Q key, 15s cooldown)
- **Level 1** (Start): Gain shield absorbing 50 damage for 3s
- **Level 2** (Wave 10): Shield increased to 100 damage, reflects 50% of blocked damage
- **Level 3** (Wave 20): Shield lasts 5s, gain movement speed while active, explodes on expiry for AOE damage
- **Level 4** (Wave 30): Shield becomes automatic when HP drops below 30%, also grants lifesteal

**Skill Tree Choices** (Pick 1 per tier during run):

**Tier 1 (Level 3)** - Choose Fighting Style:
- **Berserker Path**: +30% damage, lose 5% max HP
- **Guardian Path**: +40% max HP, -10% damage
- **Duelist Path**: +15% attack speed, gain lifesteal on critical hits

**Tier 2 (Level 8)** - Choose Offense or Defense:
- **Earthquake Slam**: Every 5th attack creates shockwave (AOE)
- **Blood Armor**: Taking damage grants temporary damage reduction stacks
- **Whirlwind**: Hold attack button to spin (continuous AOE, drains stamina)

**Tier 3 (Level 15)** - Unlock Ultimate:
- **Titan Form**: Transform for 10s (doubled size, +100% damage, immune to knockback, 60s cooldown)

**Tier 4 (Level 25)** - Choose Mastery:
- **Immortal**: Revive once per run at 50% HP
- **Execute**: Enemies below 20% HP die instantly
- **War Cry**: Buff allies in co-op (+20% damage, 10s duration, 30s cooldown)

**Knight Playstyle**: 
- Dive into crowds, sustain through lifesteal/shields
- Positioning is key (melee range = danger)
- Rewards aggressive play (more risk = more reward)
- Best for players who like to face-tank

**Weapon Synergies**:
- Fire Circle (orbiting flames while you melee)
- Thorns (reflect damage as you tank)
- Lifesteal weapons (stay alive in melee range)

---

### 🏹 ARCHER (Ranged DPS)

**Identity**: High-damage glass cannon who excels at kiting and precise positioning

**Starting Stats**:
- HP: 100 (lowest)
- Speed: 110% (fastest)
- Starting Weapon: **Longbow** (piercing arrows, long range, slower fire rate)

**Signature Ability: "Perfect Shot"** (Q key, 12s cooldown)
- **Level 1** (Start): Time slows (50%), click to fire empowered arrow (300% damage)
- **Level 2** (Wave 10): Empowered arrow pierces all enemies in line, time slow lasts longer
- **Level 3** (Wave 20): Fire up to 3 empowered arrows during slow, each can crit
- **Level 4** (Wave 30): If all arrows hit, cooldown instantly refreshes

**Skill Tree Choices**:

**Tier 1 (Level 3)** - Choose Arrow Type:
- **Explosive Arrows**: Arrows explode on hit (small AOE)
- **Poison Arrows**: Arrows apply DoT (damage over time)
- **Multi-Shot**: Fire 2 additional arrows at reduced damage

**Tier 2 (Level 8)** - Choose Mobility:
- **Dodge Roll**: Gain dodge roll ability (spacebar, 5s cooldown, i-frames)
- **Shadow Step**: Teleport short distance backward when hit (15s cooldown)
- **Wind Walker**: +20% movement speed, leave trail that slows enemies

**Tier 3 (Level 15)** - Unlock Ultimate:
- **Arrow Storm**: Fire 50 arrows in rapid succession at random nearby enemies (8s duration, 45s cooldown)

**Tier 4 (Level 25)** - Choose Mastery:
- **Headshot**: 15% chance to instantly kill non-boss enemies
- **Chain Lightning**: Arrows that hit spread electricity to 3 nearby enemies
- **Hunter's Mark**: Tagged enemy takes +50% damage from all sources (co-op synergy)

**Archer Playstyle**:
- Constant movement (kiting)
- High skill ceiling (positioning is everything)
- Burst damage potential (Perfect Shot combos)
- Punishes mistakes (low HP = one-shot risk)

**Weapon Synergies**:
- Rifle/Pistols (more projectiles = more DPS)
- Lightning Chain (ranged AOE)
- Turrets (cover your escape routes)

---

### 🔮 MAGE (Elemental Caster)

**Identity**: AOE specialist who controls the battlefield with magic

**Starting Stats**:
- HP: 120 (medium)
- Speed: 100% (average)
- Starting Weapon: **Arcane Missiles** (homing projectiles, medium damage)

**Signature Ability: "Elemental Shift"** (Q key, 18s cooldown)
- **Level 1** (Start): Toggle between Fire/Ice/Lightning stance (changes your damage type + bonus effect)
  - Fire: +20% damage, burn enemies
  - Ice: Slow enemies by 30%
  - Lightning: 10% chain to nearby enemy
- **Level 2** (Wave 10): Stance bonuses increased, switching creates explosion at your location
- **Level 3** (Wave 20): Can combine 2 elements (Fire+Ice = Steam, Fire+Lightning = Plasma, Ice+Lightning = Blizzard)
- **Level 4** (Wave 30): All 3 elements active simultaneously, passive aura damages nearby enemies

**Skill Tree Choices**:

**Tier 1 (Level 3)** - Choose Element Specialization:
- **Pyromancer**: Fire damage increased, enemies burn longer
- **Cryomancer**: Ice effects stronger, frozen enemies shatter (AOE on death)
- **Stormcaller**: Lightning chains further, movement speed increased

**Tier 2 (Level 8)** - Choose Spell Type:
- **Meteor**: Call down meteor every 20s (huge AOE, long cast time)
- **Blink**: Teleport short distance (8s cooldown, leaves AOE at start location)
- **Mana Shield**: Convert 30% damage taken into mana drain instead of HP

**Tier 3 (Level 15)** - Unlock Ultimate:
- **Cataclysm**: Create massive elemental explosion based on current stance (Fire = burn zone, Ice = freeze all, Lightning = chain storm, 60s cooldown)

**Tier 4 (Level 25)** - Choose Mastery:
- **Archmage**: All spells cost no cooldown for 10s (triggered manually, 90s cooldown)
- **Elemental Fusion**: Casting 3 different element spells creates combo explosion
- **Mana Overflow**: Max mana increased, spells generate mana orbs that heal when picked up

**Mage Playstyle**:
- Area control (zone enemies with spells)
- Element combos (rewards planning)
- Medium range (not melee, not super far)
- Strategic stance switching

**Weapon Synergies**:
- Fire Circle/Blade Storm (AOE stacks with element effects)
- Magic Bolt (scales with spell power)
- Poison Cloud (more AOE = better)

### Hero Evolution Summary

**Each hero has 4 evolution moments:**
1. **Level 3**: Choose initial specialization (defines playstyle)
2. **Level 8**: Choose build-defining ability (offense/defense/utility)
3. **Level 15**: Unlock ultimate ability (game-changing power spike)
4. **Level 25**: Choose mastery (endgame power fantasy)

**This creates branching builds:**
- Berserker Knight with Whirlwind + Execute = AOE monster
- Guardian Knight with Blood Armor + Immortal = unkillable tank
- Pyro Mage with Meteor + Archmage = nuke everything
- Cryo Mage with Blink + Elemental Fusion = combo master
- Explosive Archer with Chain Lightning + Headshot = screen-clear
- Poison Archer with Shadow Step + Hunter's Mark = boss killer

**Hundreds of viable combinations = meta diversity**

## Weapon System

### Base Weapons (6 active weapon slots max)

**Projectile Weapons**:
1. **Pistols**: Fast fire rate, medium damage, pierces 1 enemy
2. **Shotgun**: Close range, high damage, spread shot
3. **Bow**: Long range, slow fire, high damage
4. **Magic Bolt**: Homing projectiles, medium damage
5. **Rifle**: Long range, rapid fire, low damage per shot
6. **Laser**: Continuous beam, melts single targets

**Area Weapons**:
7. **Fire Circle**: Orbits player, burns enemies on contact
8. **Lightning Chain**: Strikes nearest enemy, chains to 3 more
9. **Blade Storm**: Rotating blades around player
10. **Poison Cloud**: Periodic AoE around player, DoT effect

**Deployable Weapons**:
11. **Turret**: Auto-targets and shoots enemies
12. **Mines**: Drop periodically, explode on contact
13. **Drone**: Follows player, shoots independently

### Weapon Evolution System

**Combine 2 max-level weapons → Evolved Weapon**:
- Pistols + Shotgun = **Dual Revolvers** (fast, piercing, explosive shots)
- Bow + Rifle = **Sniper Cannon** (extreme range, huge damage, slow)
- Fire Circle + Lightning Chain = **Elemental Storm** (orbiting lightning bolts)
- Blade Storm + Poison Cloud = **Toxic Blades** (poisoning orbiting blades)

**20+ evolution combinations to discover**

## Enemy Design & NPC System

### Enemy Philosophy
- **Variety over quantity**: 30+ distinct enemy types, each with unique mechanics
- **Visual clarity**: Easy to identify threats at a glance
- **Counter-play**: Every enemy has a weakness (element, positioning, timing)
- **Scaling**: Co-op mode multiplies enemy HP + count (not just HP sponges)

---

### Wave 1-10: Basic Threats

**1. Zombies** (Melee Swarm)
- Slow movement, low HP
- Spawn in groups of 10-20
- Weak to AOE, Fire element
- Teaching tool (learn movement)

**2. Runners** (Fast Melee)
- High speed, very low HP
- Beeline straight to player
- Weak to Ice (freeze stops them)
- Punishes standing still

**3. Shooters** (Ranged Basic)
- Medium HP, medium speed
- Fire projectiles every 2s
- Telegraphed shots (dodge-able)
- Weak to Archer (out-range them)

**4. Tanks** (Melee Heavy)
- High HP, slow speed
- Block projectiles from front
- Must attack from sides/back
- Weak to Knight (close range DPS)

**5. Exploders** (Suicide Units)
- Medium speed, low HP
- Run at player, explode on death (AOE damage)
- Must kill from distance
- Weak to Lightning (chain kills before they reach you)

---

### Wave 11-20: Advanced Threats

**6. Summoners** (Support Enemy)
- Low HP, slow speed
- Periodically spawn 3 Zombies
- Priority target (kill first)
- Weak to burst damage (Mage spells, Archer Perfect Shot)

**7. Teleporters** (Assassin Type)
- Medium HP, fast speed
- Teleport behind player every 5s
- Telegraphed (red indicator before teleport)
- Weak to AOE (punish their landing spot)

**8. Shielded Knights** (Directional Tank)
- High HP, medium speed
- Shield blocks ALL damage from front 180°
- Must flank or use abilities
- Weak to Knight's Whirlwind (360° attack)

**9. Necromancers** (Elite Support)
- Medium HP, slow speed
- Revive 5 nearby dead enemies every 10s
- Priority target #1
- Weak to sustained DPS (kill before they cast)

**10. Berserkers** (Ramping Threat)
- High HP, medium speed
- Gain +10% damage and speed every 5s alive
- Become extremely dangerous if ignored
- Weak to execute abilities (kill fast)

**11. Snipers** (Long-Range)
- Low HP, immobile
- Spawn at screen edge, laser-sight warning (2s), then massive damage shot
- One-shot squishy heroes if hit
- Weak to mobility (dodge the laser)

**12. Healers** (Support Cluster)
- Low HP, slow speed
- Heal nearby enemies (green beam visible)
- Always spawn near other elites
- Weak to AOE (group them then nuke)

---

### Wave 21-30: Elite Threats

**13. Shadow Stalkers** (Stealth Assassin)
- Medium HP, very fast
- Turn invisible for 3s, reappear next to player
- Audio cue (breathing sound) warns of location
- Weak to area denial (Fire Circle catches them)

**14. Juggernauts** (Super Heavy)
- Massive HP, slow speed
- Immune to crowd control (freeze, slow, stun)
- Charge attack (telegraphed, massive damage)
- Weak to kiting (dodge charge, chip damage)

**15. Witches** (Debuff Caster)
- Medium HP, medium speed
- Cast curse (reduce player damage by 50% for 5s)
- Purple projectile (dodge-able)
- Weak to Mage (element advantage)

**16. Swarm Hosts** (Spawn Boss)
- High HP, slow speed
- Constantly spawn tiny flying insects (1 HP each)
- Insects block projectiles
- Weak to AOE (clear swarm first)

**17. Mimics** (Clone Enemy)
- Medium HP, medium speed
- Copy player appearance and abilities
- Confusing in chaos
- Weak to Thorns/Reflect damage (they damage themselves)

**18. Void Reapers** (Drain Type)
- High HP, fast speed
- Melee attacks drain XP (lose progress toward next level)

- Invulnerable structure

- Destroy priority (kills portal early if possible)

### Co-Op Enemy Scaling

**2-Player Co-Op Mode Adjustments:**

**Enemy HP Scaling**:
- Base HP × 1.75 (not 2x - encourages co-op)
- Example: Zombie (50 HP solo) → (87 HP co-op)

**Enemy Count Scaling**:
- +50% more enemies per wave
- Example: Wave 5 spawns 30 Zombies solo → 45 Zombies co-op

**New Enemy Behaviors**:
- **Aggro System**: Enemies prioritize closest or lowest HP player
- **Split Aggro**: Some enemies target Player 1, some target Player 2
- **Team Mechanics**:
  - Shielded Knights require one player to bait, other to flank
  - Summoners spawn enemies targeting both players
  - Bosses have team-wipe mechanics (must coordinate dodges)

**Co-Op Exclusive Enemies**:

**"The Divider"** (Co-op only enemy):
- Creates barrier between players
- If separated for 5s, both take massive damage
- Must stay together or kill it fast
- Forces coordination

**"Link Shaman"** (Co-op only elite):
- Links both players with chain
- If players separate beyond chain length, both are stunned
- Must move together until chain breaks (10s or kill shaman)

**Co-Op Boss Modifiers**:
- Bosses gain "Divide and Conquer" mechanic
- Example: Titan charges one player while spawning adds near the other
- Example: Shadow Assassin clones target different players
- Example: Rift Lord assigns each player a different element weakness

**Co-Op Difficulty Tiers**:
- **Normal Co-op**: HP × 1.75, Count × 1.5
- **Hard Co-op**: HP × 2.0, Count × 2.0, elite enemies spawn earlier
- **Nightmare Co-op**: HP × 2.5, Count × 2.5, permadeath (one player dies = both fail)

**Co-Op Rewards**:
- Separate leaderboard (solo vs co-op)
- Exclusive co-op cosmetics (both players get if they win together)
- "Dynamic Duo" achievements (beat wave 30 together)
- Guild points (co-op runs count more for guild ranking)

**Co-Op Synergies**:
- Knight taunts enemies → Archer free DPS from safety
- Mage freezes enemies → Knight shatters them for bonus damage
- Archer marks target → Mage nukes for +50% damage
- Ultimate combos (both ults at same time = screen wipe)
